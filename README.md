# September Market Risk Analyzer

A stock-market analysis program focused on September seasonality and market-cycle risk.

## Stocks

AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, META, IBM, AMZN, GOOGL, AMD, MU

## Analysis

- September daily decline probabilities

- Historical market seasonality

- 40-trading-day cycle

- 100-trading-day cycle

- 300+ trading-day cycle

- Market breadth

- Volatility and VIX term structure

- Market-top risk signals

## Goal

Identify historically high-risk September trading days and combine them with current market indicators to create a Market Risk Score.
